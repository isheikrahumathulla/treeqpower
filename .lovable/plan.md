# TreeQ Power homepage using Zoho’s layout system

## Goal
Rebuild the TreeQ Power homepage to closely match the current Zoho homepage’s structure, proportions, visual rhythm, and responsive behavior, while retaining TreeQ Power’s identity and approved content.

Zoho will control the presentation; TreeQ Power will control the brand and information. Zoho names, copy, imagery, logos, statistics, customer claims, and proprietary graphics will not be copied.

## Visual direction
- White foundation with TreeQ green `#2ba645` as the primary action color, orange `#ff4e07` for small emphasis details, and black `#0d0d0d` for typography and strong contrast.
- Sora headings and Manrope body copy.
- Structured grid composition with Zoho-like bold headings, thin divider rules, compact cards, alternating white and dark sections, and direct calls to action.

## Homepage structure
1. **Header** — compact white navigation, TreeQ logo, dropdown menus based on the approved TreeQ sitemap, enquiry action, and mobile menu.
2. **Opening section** — centered editorial headline and short supporting statement, followed by a large TreeQ capability showcase instead of Zoho’s application grid.
3. **Featured capabilities** — one highlighted TreeQ discipline beside a modular grid for Engineering, Field & Maintenance, Testing & Inspection, Sustainability, and Asset Integrity.
4. **Integrated solutions feature** — Zoho One–style split section adapted to Power Systems, Automation & Control, Power Quality, and Building Technology.
5. **Clients and projects** — preserve clearly labelled placeholders until verified logos, testimonials, and project data are supplied.
6. **Purpose and delivery stories** — large visual storytelling panels for TreeQ’s integrated lifecycle approach and operating environments.
7. **Technical values** — four structured blocks covering engineering rigor, coordinated delivery, safety focus, and lifecycle support, using only supported claims.
8. **Documented technical scope** — present verified voltage and service-scope information without turning it into unsupported company-performance statistics.
9. **FAQ and enquiry** — maintain the existing technical FAQ and contact information, then close with a concise Zoho-like call-to-action band and structured footer.

## Interaction and responsive behavior
- Desktop dropdown navigation and a full mobile navigation panel.
- Subtle section reveals, card emphasis, button transitions, and horizontal movement where appropriate.
- Mobile-specific stacking and type sizing that preserve the Zoho-inspired hierarchy without overflow.
- Reduced-motion support, keyboard accessibility, and clear focus states.

## Content safeguards
- Retain TreeQ Power’s existing approved technical content and contact details marked for confirmation.
- Keep project imagery, client logos, testimonials, certifications, and unverified claims as explicit placeholders.
- Do not use Zoho assets or fabricate TreeQ facts.

## Verification
- Compare the completed desktop section order and proportions against Zoho’s homepage.
- Test mobile navigation, capability interactions, FAQ, contact form state, keyboard navigation, overflow, and console errors.
- Confirm route metadata remains TreeQ-specific and the final build succeeds.
