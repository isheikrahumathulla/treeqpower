# Home Page — UI Layout Options Only

## 1. Repetition Audit

- **Bordered card grids repeat most:** Core Capabilities, Why TreeQ Power, Our Approach, and Resources all use evenly sized cells with icon/number, heading, copy, and hover lift.
- **Image + text splits repeat:** About, Featured Technical Solutions, Asset Integrity, Sustainability, and the FAQ introduction all use two-column compositions.
- **Image-card collections repeat:** Services and Solutions use the same horizontal card rail; Industries uses another repeated image-card set.
- **Section openings repeat:** Most sections begin with the same eyebrow, large heading, short introduction, then a grid.
- **Motion repeats:** Nearly every section uses one section-level fade-up; most cards then rely on the same lift or image-zoom hover.
- **No statistics issue on the current Home page:** there is no live statistics section, so one should not be introduced without verified data.

## 2. Section-by-Section Options

### Capability Strip

**Option 1 — Technical Index Rail**  
**Layout:** Keep all eight disciplines in one compact horizontal index with a persistent active marker and condensed labels.  
**Animation:** The marker glides between items; the rail scrolls with snap on smaller screens.  
**Why:** Preserves fast navigation while making the strip feel like an engineered control surface rather than another card grid.

**Option 2 — Expanding Discipline Bar**  
**Layout:** Show eight narrow columns; the hovered or focused discipline expands to reveal a one-line scope while neighbors compress.  
**Animation:** Smooth width interpolation and icon-to-label emphasis; tap opens the item on mobile.  
**Why:** Adds useful depth without adding content or taking much vertical space.

**Option 3 — Lifecycle Ribbon**  
**Layout:** Arrange the disciplines along one continuous line grouped by engineering, execution, verification, and lifecycle support.  
**Animation:** The line draws on entry and each discipline activates in sequence.  
**Why:** Communicates integration more clearly than eight visually equal boxes.

### About TreeQ Power

**Option 1 — Editorial Manifesto**  
**Layout:** Use a large left-aligned statement across the page, followed by three narrow text columns and one inset field photograph.  
**Animation:** Copy reveals line-by-line; the photograph rises slightly later.  
**Why:** Breaks the repeated image/text split while giving the company positioning greater authority.

**Option 2 — Sticky Lifecycle Story**  
**Layout:** Keep the heading and photograph sticky on the left while the three existing paragraphs scroll as engineering, execution, and lifecycle chapters on the right.  
**Animation:** The image caption and active chapter change as each paragraph enters view.  
**Why:** Converts dense company copy into a paced narrative without changing its facts.

**Option 3 — Full-Width Image Ledger**  
**Layout:** Place a wide industrial photograph across the section with the company statement above and three factual scope rows below.  
**Animation:** A restrained image crop shift accompanies staggered row reveals.  
**Why:** Creates a strong visual pause before the capability-heavy sections.

### Core Capabilities

**Option 1 — Interactive Capability Matrix**  
**Layout:** Use a compact 2×4 matrix; selecting a discipline opens one shared detail panel with its description and link.  
**Animation:** The active cell highlights while the detail panel crossfades.  
**Why:** Keeps all eight disciplines scannable but avoids eight equally dense cards.

**Option 2 — Bento Capability System**  
**Layout:** Give broader disciplines larger cells and supporting disciplines smaller cells within a disciplined asymmetric grid.  
**Animation:** Sequential reveal follows the grid hierarchy; hover exposes the direct link.  
**Why:** Establishes visual priority while retaining the complete approved capability set.

**Option 3 — Horizontal Technical Chapters**  
**Layout:** Present one large capability at a time with oversized numbering, concise copy, and a persistent eight-item index.  
**Animation:** Scroll-snap or arrow navigation transitions between chapters.  
**Why:** Gives each technical discipline more presence and introduces a premium slide-based moment.

### Why TreeQ Power

**Option 1 — Sticky Proof Stack**  
**Layout:** Keep the field-engineering image and section heading sticky while six reasons move past as numbered editorial statements.  
**Animation:** The active reason sharpens the image and highlights its number.  
**Why:** Turns six similar boxes into a convincing, sequential argument.

**Option 2 — Radial Capability Map**  
**Layout:** Place “Engineering + Execution + Lifecycle” at the center with six reasons arranged around it as linked principles.

**Animation:** Rows enter from alternating sides with restrained distance.  
**Why:** Feels editorial and technical while avoiding another 3×2 grid.

## 3. Global Layout Recommendation

Use a controlled mix rather than choosing every interactive option:

- **Capability Strip:** Lifecycle Ribbon
- **About:** Editorial Manifesto
- **Core Capabilities:** Interactive Capability Matrix
- **Why TreeQ Power:** Sticky Proof Stack
- **Services:** Service Explorer
- **Solutions:** Systems Architecture Map
- **Industries:** Cinematic Industry Accordion
- **Approach:** Connected Process Line
- **Technical Solutions:** Technical Specification Board
- **Asset Integrity:** Condition-to-Decision Story
- **Sustainability:** Image-Led Statement
- **Resources:** Editorial Resource Shelf
- **FAQ:** Refined Numbered Editorial Accordion
- **Final Contact:** Editorial Contact Band

This distribution keeps TreeQ’s typography, colors, spacing, imagery, and motion language consistent while alternating five storytelling modes: editorial, interactive index, process diagram, image-led immersion, and technical data presentation. Use no more than two sticky/scroll-driven sections on the page, keep one strong horizontal interaction, and retain restrained fade transitions elsewhere so the experience remains premium rather than over-animated.

**Reference note:** Superlógica’s live page blocked automated inspection during this review. The recommendations therefore use the established reference qualities already defined for this project—editorial scale, sticky storytelling, horizontal movement, interactive panels, refined accordions, and restrained transitions—without copying its content or branding.
